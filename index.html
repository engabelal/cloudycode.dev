My process

How I work

I turn risk and requirements into secure, operable platforms—with clear decisions, visible progress, and evidence at every phase.

  1. Phase 01

    Discover & frame

    Clarify outcomes, constraints, risk, and the current operating reality before selecting tools or prescribing architecture.

    CONTEXT · RISK · SCOPE
  2. Phase 02

    Architect & align

    Define system boundaries, delivery paths, security controls, and decisions the whole team can understand and operate.

    DESIGN · CONTROL · DECIDE

  4. Phase 04

    Launch & improve

    RELEASE · OBSERVE · EVOLVE
